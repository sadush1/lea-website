Our mission

Little explorers can make a big difference.

Lea teaches children that Albania's nature is home to birds, animals, plants and people — and that caring for it is everyone's responsibility.

Explore it. Love it. Protect it.

As Lea's world grows, we hope to support real nature education and conservation efforts in Albania. Partnerships will be announced here once they exist.

For parents

More than a story. A first connection with Albania.

Lea helps children discover geography, nature, history, culture, language and environmental responsibility — without making it feel like homework.

The stories are written primarily for little explorers aged 3–8, while parents and grandparents can enjoy rediscovering Albania right alongside them.
